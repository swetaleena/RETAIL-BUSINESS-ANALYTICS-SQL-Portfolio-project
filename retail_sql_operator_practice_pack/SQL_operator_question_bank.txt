SQL OPERATOR QUESTION BANK
Business context: A retailer sells electronics, furniture, lifestyle and stationery products.
Use the supplied CSV tables. Write SQL queries; do not use the examples as answers.

A. ARITHMETIC OPERATORS
1. (+) The finance team wants to estimate the revised list price after adding a flat ₹250 handling charge. Display product name, list price and revised price.
2. (-) Calculate the gross profit per unit for every product using list_price minus cost_price.
3. (*) Calculate the gross sales value for each order line using quantity multiplied by unit_price.
4. (/) Calculate the average selling price per unit for each order line using line value divided by quantity. Avoid division by zero.
5. (%) The warehouse wants to identify products whose stock quantity leaves a remainder of 0 when divided into packs of 10. Show product and stock quantity.

B. COMPARISON OPERATORS
6. (=) Find all products in the Electronics category.
7. (>) Find products whose list price is greater than ₹10,000.
8. (<) Find products whose stock quantity is below 20.
9. (>=) Find purchase orders whose quantity is at least 50.
10. (<=) Find products whose cost price is ₹1,000 or less.
11. (<>) Find orders whose status is not Delivered.

C. COMPOUND ASSIGNMENT OPERATORS – DIALECT AWARENESS
These are shown on the W3Schools page but are not valid MySQL compound assignment syntax.
12. (+=) A stock controller wants to add 10 units to every active product. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE.
13. (-=) Reduce stock by 3 units for products in the Lifestyle category. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE.
14. (*=) Increase list prices by 5% for Furniture products. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE.
15. (/=) Divide a temporary numeric allowance column by 2. Explain why a suitable numeric column is needed; do not update list_price for this exercise.
16. (%=) Set a temporary batch number to its remainder when divided by 5. Write the MySQL-compatible assignment expression.
17. (&=) Keep only permission bits 1 and 4 in permission_mask. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE expression.
18. (^=) Toggle permission bits represented by mask 2. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE expression.
19. (|=) Add the approval permission bit (8) to permission_mask without removing existing permissions. Write the MySQL-compatible UPDATE expression.
For questions 12–19, use a transaction and ROLLBACK after testing. Do not execute against production data.

D. BITWISE OPERATORS
Permission bit meanings: 1=View, 2=Create, 4=Edit, 8=Approve.
20. (&) Find employees who have the Edit permission (bit 4).
21. (|) For each employee, show the permission mask after adding the Approve bit (8), without changing the stored data.
22. (^) Show the result of toggling the Create permission bit (2) for each employee.
23. (~) Display the bitwise NOT of each employee's permission mask. Explain why the result may be negative in MySQL's signed integer representation.
Optional extension: use shifts (<< and >>) to demonstrate how permission bits are positioned.

E. LOGICAL OPERATORS
24. (ALL) Find products priced lower than every product in category_id 2.
25. (AND) Find active Electronics products with stock below 50.
26. (ANY) Find products priced higher than at least one product supplied by supplier 302. Use a subquery.
27. (BETWEEN) Find orders placed between 1 February 2025 and 31 March 2025, inclusive.
28. (EXISTS) Find suppliers who have at least one purchase order.
29. (IN) Find products that appear in purchase orders from supplier 301 or supplier 302.
30. (LIKE) Find customers whose names begin with 'S'.
31. (NOT) Find products that are not in the Furniture category.
32. (OR) Find orders paid by UPI or Wallet.
33. (SOME) Find products priced higher than SOME prices of products in category_id 3. Compare the result with ANY.

F. INTEGRATED BUSINESS QUESTIONS
34. Calculate order-line revenue after discount and show only lines with net value above ₹5,000.
35. Find customers who have placed at least one Delivered order and live in Odisha or Maharashtra.
36. Identify suppliers with at least one purchase order that has not yet been received.
37. Find products that are active, have stock below 20, and cost more than ₹1,000.
38. Find each product's gross margin percentage and flag products below 20%.
39. Find the total procurement spend by supplier and sort from highest to lowest.
40. Identify products whose current stock is an exact multiple of 5 and whose list price is between ₹500 and ₹5,000.

STUDENT SUBMISSION EXPECTATION
For every question, submit:
1. SQL query
2. A one-sentence explanation of the logic
3. A screenshot or copied result set
4. One business observation from the output

INSTRUCTOR NOTE
The compound operator questions are conceptual translation exercises. MySQL uses assignments such as:
UPDATE products SET stock_qty = stock_qty + 10;
UPDATE employee_access SET permission_mask = permission_mask | 8;
Use START TRANSACTION; ... ROLLBACK; for safe practice.
